**Ideal travel window:** October – March (dry, pleasant weather).  

Below is a ready‑to‑follow day‑by‑day plan, plus transport, accommodation, food & budget pointers. Feel free to swap nights or add a day‑trip if you have extra time.

## DETAILED DAY‑BY‑DAY ITINERARY  

### **Day 1 – Arrival & Exploring Kochi**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **Morning** | Fly into **Cochin International Airport (COK)**. (If you’re coming from China, look for connecting flights via major hubs – e.g., Delhi, Mumbai, Singapore, or Dubai.) |
| **Transfer** | Pre‑book a **airport taxi / app‑based car (Ola/ Uber)** to your hotel (≈ 30 min). |
| **Check‑in** | **Budget‑friendly**: *Treebo Trend The Qasr* (Fort Kochi) – clean, central, ~₹2,500/night. |
| **Mid‑day** | Walk to **Fort Kochi**: Dutch Palace (Mattancherry), St. Francis Church, Jew Town & the iconic **Chinese Fishing Nets**. Grab a **Kerala lunch** at *Kashi Art Café* (fish fry, appam & stew). |
| **Afternoon** | Explore **Willingdon Island** (Marine Drive) and the **Kerala Folklore Museum**. |
| **Evening** | Watch a **Kathakali** performance at *Kerala Kathakali Centre* (≈ ₹500 per ticket). |
| **Dinner** | Try *Ginger House* for authentic Kerala cuisine. |
| **Overnight** | Kochi |

---

### **Day 2 – Hill‑top Munnar**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **07:00** | Breakfast, checkout. |
| **07:30** | Private **car/van** (or hired taxi) to **Munnar** – 4 h scenic drive through the Western Ghats (stop at *Cheeyappara & Valara* waterfalls). |
| **12:00** | Check‑in **budget hotel** – *Hotel Green View* (~₹2,200/night) or a **homestay** for a local feel. |
| **13:00** | Lunch at *Rapsy Restaurant* (South Indian thali). |
| **14:30** | **Tea Plantation** walk – *Kolukkumalai* (optional, requires 2‑hour hike) or **Tea Museum**. |
| **17:00** | **Eravikulam National Park** – see the endangered **Nilgiri Tahr** and the rolling **Anamudi** peak. (Entry ₹300 + guide ₹200). |
| **Evening** | Relax at the hotel, enjoy a **bonfire** (many homestays arrange). |
| **Dinner** | Homemade Kerala dinner (often included in homestays). |
| **Overnight** | Munnar |

---

### **Day 3 – Wildlife & Spice in Thekkady**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **08:00** | Breakfast, checkout. |
| **08:30** | Drive to **Thekkady** (≈ 3 h). |
| **12:00** | Check‑in **budget resort** – *Green View Resort* (~₹2,800/night). |
| **13:00** | Lunch – *Hotel Spice Garden* (try the **Kerala fish curry**). |
| **14:30** | **Periyar Wildlife Sanctuary** – boat safari on Periyar Lake (₹500 per person) to spot elephants, bison & birds. |
| **17:00** | **Spice Plantation Tour** – learn about cardamom, pepper, cinnamon (often free with tea). |
| **Evening** | **Bamboo rafting** or **Nature walk** (optional). |
| **Dinner** | Resort restaurant (try **Kerala beef fry** or **paneer tikka** for vegetarians). |
| **Overnight** | Thekkady |

---

### **Day 4 – Backwaters & Birdlife – Kumarakom**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **08:00** | Breakfast, checkout. |
| **08:30** | Road to **Kumarakom** (≈ 3 h). |
| **12:00** | Check‑in **budget lakeside hotel** – *Kumarakom Lake Resort* (dorm‑style rooms ~₹2,500) or *The Lakeside Retreat* (₹3,000). |
| **13:00** | Lunch – *Hotel Kumarakom* (fresh fish & rice). |
| **14:30** | **Kumarakom Bird Sanctuary** – guided walk (₹150 entry). Spot migratory birds (storks, kingfishers). |
| **17:00** | Sunset **boat cruise** on Vembanad Lake (₹600 per person). |
| **Dinner** | Lakeside dinner with **Karimeen (Pearl Spot) fry**. |
| **Overnight** | Kumarakom |

---

### **Day 5 – Houseboat Night on the Backwaters (Alleppey)**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **08:00** | Early breakfast, checkout. |
| **08:30** | Short 1.5 h drive to **Alleppey (Alappuzha)**. |
| **10:00** | Board a **traditional houseboat** (₹8,000‑₹12,000 per night for 2‑4 pax, includes meals). |
| **10:30** | Cruise through the **Vembanad‑Kumarakom** backwaters: pass paddy fields, coconut groves, villages. |
| **13:00** | **Lunch on board** – Kerala Sadya (12‑dish vegetarian feast). |
| **16:00** | Stop at a **coir‑making village** for a quick demo. |
| **Evening** | Sunset on the lake, **cultural performance** (folk songs). |
| **Dinner** | Sadya continues; try **payasam** for dessert. |
| **Overnight** | Houseboat (you’ll be sleeping on the boat!). |

---

### **Day 6 – Cliffside Varkala**
| Time | Activity |
|------|----------|
| **07:00** | Early breakfast on the boat, disembark at Alleppey jetty. |
| **07:30** | Private transfer to **Varkala** (≈ 3 h). |
| **11:00** | Check‑in **budget hotel** – *Zostel Varkala* (dorms ~₹800, private rooms ~₹2,200) or *The Gateway Hotel* (~₹3,500). |
| **12:30** | Lunch at *Clafouti* (sea‑food thali). |
| **14:00** | **Varkala Cliff** walk – stunning sea‑view cafés, shops, and the **Janardhana Swamy Temple** (one of the 108 Shiva temples). |
| **16:30** | **Beach time** – swim, try **surfing** or **parasailing** (≈ ₹1,200). |
| **Evening** | Sunset at **Papanasam Beach** (famous for its “holy dip”). |
| **Dinner** | Beachside shacks – **Kappa (tapioca) & fish curry**. |
| **Overnight** | Varkala |

| **10:00** | Checkout & drive back to **Kochi International Airport** (≈ 4 h) **or** to **Trivandrum International Airport** (≈ 2 h) depending on your flight. |
| **12:30** | If time permits, quick stop at **Marine Drive** for a coffee. |

## LOGISTICS & PRACTICAL TIPS  

| Item | Recommendation |
|------|----------------|
| **Flights** | Search for the cheapest inbound flight to **Cochin (COK)** or **Trivandrum (TRV)**. Major carriers (Air India, Emirates, Qatar, Singapore Airlines) have daily connections from most Asian & European hubs. |
| **Visas** | Indian e‑Tourist Visa (e‑Visa) is available for Chinese, US, EU, etc. Apply 4‑7 days before travel. |
| **Transport** | Hire a **private driver with a comfortable sedan (Toyota Innova/Etios)** for the whole week (≈ ₹5,000‑₹6,000 per day, fuel extra). This gives flexibility and saves time vs. public buses. |
| **Accommodation** | Budget‑midrange hotels 2‑star to 3‑star cost ₹2,000‑₹4,000 per night. Houseboat is the splurge night. Book via **Booking.com**, **MakeMyTrip**, or directly on the property’s website. |
| **Food budget** | ₹300‑₹500 per meal at mid‑range restaurants; street‑food & local cafés can be as low as ₹150. |
| **Total estimated cost (per person, solo)** | **Flights**: US$600‑$900 (varies) <br> **Accommodation**: ₹22,000‑₹30,000 <br> **Transport (private car)**: ₹30,000‑₹35,000 (shared among travelers) <br> **Activities & entry fees**: ₹5,000‑₹7,000 <br> **Food**: ₹7,000‑₹9,000 <br> **Overall**: **≈ US$1,200‑$1,500** (mid‑range) |
| **What to pack** | Light cotton clothing, a couple of warm layers for Munnar (it can be 10‑15 °C), comfortable walking shoes, swimwear, sunscreen, mosquito repellent, a small day‑pack, reusable water bottle. |
| **Health & safety** | Drink bottled or filtered water. Carry basic meds (pain reliever, anti‑diarrheal, motion‑sickness). Travel insurance covering medical evacuation is advisable. |
| **Cultural etiquette** | Dress modestly when visiting temples (cover shoulders & knees). Remove shoes before entering. Tipping 5‑10 % in restaurants & for guides is appreciated. |
| **Connectivity** | Purchase a **local SIM (Airtel/ Jio/ Vi)** at the airport – ₹300 for 10 GB data (valid 30 days). |
